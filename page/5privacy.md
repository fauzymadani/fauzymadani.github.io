This site is a static website built with Jekyll and hosted through GitHub Pages.
It does not provide accounts, comments, contact forms, or user-generated
content.

## Analytics

This site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to understand
general traffic, such as page views and referring pages. GoatCounter is
privacy-focused and does not use advertising profiles or tracking cookies.

Analytics requests may still be blocked by browser extensions, private
browsing settings, or network filters. You can read more about GoatCounter's
approach in its [privacy documentation](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

## External services

Pages may load resources from external services, including:

* Google Fonts for Source Sans 3 and JetBrains Mono.
* cdnjs for Font Awesome interface icons.
* jsDelivr for the client-side search library on the search page.
* GoatCounter for privacy-focused, aggregate analytics.
* MathJax from cdnjs on posts that explicitly enable mathematical notation.

These services may receive a request containing your IP address and browser
information as part of serving or processing the requested resource. The site
does not sell personal information or use advertising networks.

## Email address

The public email link is assembled by JavaScript instead of being written as a
plain `mailto:` URL in the HTML. This reduces collection by simple scrapers,
but it is not a security guarantee. If you email me, your email address and
message will be visible to me and may be retained as part of normal
correspondence.

Posts may link to external websites. Their privacy practices are separate from
