I’m Fauzy Madani, a software developer and curious generalist. This is my
personal notebook for ideas, experiments, and things I want to remember.

The site is intentionally small and straightforward. I prefer writing that
explains how something works, records the decisions behind it, and remains
useful after the original problem has been forgotten.

## What I write about
Most of the notes here are related to software, personal workflows, Linux,
Emacs, games, and practical experiments. Some entries are polished guides;
others are short working notes or records of a solution.
I also use this space for academic and research-related workflows: reading,
organizing references, managing files, and making technical work reproducible.

## How I work
I value simple tools, plain text, automation, and documentation that can be
understood by a future version of myself. I do not try to make every project
perfect before publishing it. A useful imperfect note is often better than a
perfect note that never exists.
